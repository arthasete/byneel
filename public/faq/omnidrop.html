OmniDrop FAQ

Questions & Answers about secure file sharing

FAQ Getting Started Privacy Policy
Getting Started
Do I need to create an account to share files? ▼
No! OmniDrop is designed to work without any account creation. Simply select your files, generate a link, and share it. We don't require email addresses, passwords, or any personal information to get started.
How do I share files with OmniDrop? ▼
It's simple: (1) Open OmniDrop and tap "Select Files", (2) Choose the files you want to share, (3) Tap "Generate Link" to create a unique shareable URL, (4) Copy the link and send it via email, messenger, or any communication method. Recipients can download without needing an account.
What file types can I share? ▼
OmniDrop supports any file type: documents (PDF, Word, Excel), images (JPG, PNG, GIF), videos (MP4, MOV), audio (MP3, WAV), archives (ZIP, RAR), and more. The only limit is the file size supported by your plan (2GB for free, 20GB for premium).
How large can my files be? ▼
Free accounts can share files up to 2GB each with 1GB total storage. Premium accounts support up to 20GB per file with 1TB total storage. Perfect for large videos, archives, and professional documents.
Sharing & Links
How long are share links active? ▼
Free links expire in 30 days by default. Premium users can customize expiration from 1 day to 1 year. Once a link expires or the download limit is reached, the file becomes inaccessible.
Can I set a download limit on my links? ▼
Yes! Free accounts can set limits up to 10 downloads per link. Premium accounts have unlimited downloads. Once the limit is reached, the link stops working automatically.
Can I password-protect my files? ▼
Yes. When creating a share link, you can optionally add a password. Recipients must enter the password before accessing or downloading the file. This adds an extra layer of security for sensitive documents.
Can I see who downloaded my files? ▼
Yes. OmniDrop tracks download activity for your shares, showing timestamps of when downloads occurred. This helps you verify that recipients have received your files.
Can I share multiple files in one link? ▼
Absolutely! Select multiple files during upload and they'll be packaged together in a single share link. Large batches are automatically compressed for easier download.
Security & Privacy
Is my data really encrypted? ▼
Yes. OmniDrop uses AES-256 encryption for files and TLS 1.3 for all data in transit. Files are encrypted on your device before upload, meaning only you and your recipients have access to the content.
Does OmniDrop track my activity? ▼
No. OmniDrop is privacy-first. We don't use cookies, analytics, or third-party trackers. We only track download activity on your own shares (so you know when files were accessed). Your personal browsing and file history remain private.
Where are my files stored? ▼
All OmniDrop files are stored on secure servers in the EU (Frankfurt), ensuring GDPR compliance. This means your data stays within the European data protection framework.
What happens to my files after they expire? ▼
Files are automatically and permanently deleted from our servers once the expiration date passes or the download limit is reached. There's no way to recover them after deletion.
Is OmniDrop GDPR compliant? ▼
Yes. OmniDrop is fully GDPR compliant with data storage in the EU and transparent privacy practices. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time.

Technical Issues
Why is my upload slow? ▼
Upload speed depends on your internet connection and file size. Large files naturally take longer. If you're experiencing consistently slow uploads, try: (1) Checking your internet speed, (2) Uploading at a different time, (3) Compressing large files first, or (4) Contacting support@byneel.com.
What should I do if a download fails? ▼
Failed downloads are usually due to connection issues. Try: (1) Check your internet connection, (2) Try again in a few minutes, (3) Use a different browser or device, (4) Ensure the link hasn't expired. Contact support if the issue persists.
Does OmniDrop work on mobile and desktop? ▼
Yes! OmniDrop works on iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, and web browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). You can share files from any device and recipients can download on any platform.
Do I need to install OmniDrop to share files? ▼
The web version works in your browser without installation. The mobile apps (iOS/Android) can be installed from the App Store and Google Play for easier access and offline features.
