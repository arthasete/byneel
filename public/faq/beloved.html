What is Beloved?
Is Beloved a dating app? ▼
No. Beloved is an interactive conversational experience where you chat with four AI characters who are designed to feel emotionally present and memorable. It's not about matching with people—it's about exploring connection with characters who are fully realized, with their own stories and emotional depth.
What makes Beloved different from other chat apps? ▼
Beloved has four deeply-written characters with distinct personalities, backstories, and emotional arcs. Each one uses advanced AI (Claude) to respond thoughtfully. The app includes a memory system so characters remember what you've told them, building a genuine sense of relationship over time. There's no swiping, no algorithm—just meaningful conversation.
How is Beloved different from ChatGPT or other AI assistants? ▼
Beloved isn't a general-purpose assistant. Each character has a specific personality, voice, and emotional framework. Kael is cold and guarded. Noah is warm and devoted. Jin is poetic and mysterious. Marco is direct and passionate. They respond not just to answer questions, but to engage emotionally. It's designed for connection, not information.
Getting Started
How do I download Beloved? ▼
Beloved is available on iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play). Search for "Beloved" or visit byneel.com/beloved. The app is free to download with a free tier included.
Do I need an account? ▼
You can use Beloved without an account to explore characters. However, creating a free account lets you save your conversations, sync across devices, and unlock all features. Account creation requires only an email and password.
Who are the four characters? ▼
Kael (29) — Former special forces officer, now cold-hearted CEO. Guarded, intense, protective.

Noah (27) — ER doctor who falls first. Warm, devoted, genuine despite exhaustion.

Jin (31) — Concert pianist haunted by past heartbreak. Poetic, mysterious, slowly opening up.

Marco (28) — Pro footballer. Direct, passionate, emotionally raw and honest.
Can I switch between characters? ▼
Yes. The home screen shows all four characters. Each one has a separate conversation history with you. You can chat with them in any order, and each conversation develops independently. Kael won't know you talked to Noah earlier (unless you tell him).
Mana & Subscriptions
What is the Mana system? ▼
Mana is Beloved's energy currency. Each message costs a certain amount of mana depending on the character and complexity of your message. The mana system encourages meaningful conversation over rapid chatting. It refills daily, and premium subscriptions provide much larger daily allowances.
How much mana do I get daily? ▼
Free tier: 15 mana per day (max 30).
Beloved (Premium): 100 mana per day (max 1,000).
Beloved Pro: 300 mana per day (max 3,000).
How much mana does a message cost? ▼
Messages cost 1-3 mana depending on character and message type. Simple messages to Noah or Marco cost 1 mana. Complex, emotional messages or any message to Kael or Jin costs 3 mana. Premium subscribers get discounted rates.
Can I earn mana without paying? ▼
Yes! The free tier includes daily refills (15 mana) and watch ads for 5 bonus mana (up to 3 times daily). This gives free users about 30 mana per day if they watch ads. Premium removes this limitation.
What subscriptions are available? ▼
Free: $0 — 15 mana/day, watch ads for bonuses.
Beloved: $9.99/month — 100 mana/day, ad-free, exclusive features.
Beloved Pro: $14.99/month — 300 mana/day, all Beloved features plus character customization.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime? ▼
Yes, absolutely. No long-term commitment. You can cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings. Your current month's access will remain active until the billing date.
Conversations & Memory
Do characters remember previous conversations? ▼
Yes! Beloved has a memory system. Every 10 messages, the app summarizes your conversation and stores key details (emotions, preferences, important moments). Characters can access these memories, making them feel like they actually know you over time.
Can I see my full conversation history? ▼
Yes. Each character's conversation is stored locally on your device (and on your account if you're logged in). You can scroll back to review past messages anytime.
Can I clear a character's memory and start fresh? ▼
Yes. In Settings, you can "Reset Character" for any of the four. This deletes all messages and memories with that character and starts a fresh conversation. The character won't remember previous interactions.
What if I'm not happy with a character's response? ▼
Characters respond based on AI and your conversation history. If a response doesn't feel right: (1) Continue the conversation—they'll adapt. (2) Try a different character. (3) Check if the character is staying true to their personality. (4) Contact support if something feels broken.
Privacy & Safety
Is my data private? ▼
Yes. Your conversations are encrypted and stored on Supabase servers in the EU (Frankfurt). We use Row-Level Security, meaning only you can access your data. We don't sell data or use conversations for ads.
Can I use Face ID or Touch ID to lock Beloved? ▼
Yes! In Settings, you can enable biometric authentication. This requires Face ID (iOS) or fingerprint (Android/iOS) to open the app, adding an extra privacy layer.
Can I delete my account and data? ▼
Yes, absolutely. In Settings > Account > Delete Account, you can permanently delete your account and all associated data. This includes messages, memories, and personal information. This action cannot be undone.
Is Beloved GDPR compliant? ▼
Yes. Beloved follows all GDPR requirements: data stored in the EU, transparent privacy practices, user rights to access/delete data, and no unauthorized third-party sharing. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
Is this appropriate for younger users? ▼
Beloved is rated 12+, though we recommend it for ages 16+. It contains simulated romantic conversations and emotional themes. It's designed for users curious about relationships and emotional connection. Parents should review the content guidelines.
Technical & Troubleshooting
Can I use Beloved on multiple devices? ▼
Yes. If you create an account, your conversations sync across your devices. Log in on your phone and tablet, and both will have access to your message history and memories.
What if I'm offline? ▼
Beloved caches your recent messages locally, so you can read conversation history offline. However, sending new messages requires an internet connection to reach the AI.
Why aren't characters responding? ▼
Check: (1) Do you have internet connection? (2) Do you have enough mana? (3) Is the app up to date? (4) Try closing and reopening the app. If issues persist, contact support@byneel.com.
Will Beloved come to web or other platforms? ▼
Web and desktop apps are in development for future releases. For now, Beloved is mobile-only (iOS and Android) to keep the experience intimate and focused.
Support & Feedback
How do I contact support? ▼
Email support@byneel.com. We typically respond within 24 hours for free users, 2-4 hours for premium users. You can also use the in-app support button (Help in Settings).
Can I suggest features or improvements? ▼
Absolutely! We'd love your feedback. Email support@byneel.com or use the "Send Feedback" option in Settings. We actively consider user suggestions for updates.
Will there be more characters? ▼
Future updates may include additional characters. We're focused on depth over breadth—making the four existing characters incredibly rich and meaningful rather than adding many shallow ones.
