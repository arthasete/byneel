Installation & Setup

Step 1: Download OmniDrop

OmniDrop is available on multiple platforms:

  • iOS: Search for "OmniDrop" in the App Store
  • Android: Search for "OmniDrop" in Google Play
  • Web: Visit app.omnidrop.com in any modern browser
  • Mac/Windows: Use the web version or download the native app

Step 2: Launch the App

Open OmniDrop after installation. The app opens to the main screen ready to share files immediately—no account required!

Step 3: Optional—Create an Account

To save your share history and access files across devices, tap "Create Account" and enter:

  • Email address
  • Password
  • Confirm password

Your account is encrypted and private. We only use your email for login recovery.

💡 Tip: You can share files without an account. Create one later if you want to access your share history or sync across devices.

Sharing Files in 4 Steps

Step 1: Tap "Select Files"

On the main screen, tap the large blue button labeled "Select Files." This opens your device's file picker.

Step 2: Choose Your Files

Navigate to the files you want to share:

  • Browse folders on your device
  • Tap to select individual files
  • Hold Ctrl (Windows) or Cmd (Mac) to select multiple files
  • On mobile, tap files to add them to your selection

You can select up to 2GB per file (free) or 20GB (premium).

Step 3: Configure Share Options

After selecting files, you'll see a "Share Settings" screen:

  • Expiration: Set when the link expires (default: 30 days for free)
  • Download Limit: Max downloads before the link stops working (default: 10 for free)
  • Password: Optional—require a password for access
  • Message: Add a note for recipients

Premium users can customize expiration up to 1 year and allow unlimited downloads.

Step 4: Generate & Share Link

Tap "Generate Link" and you'll instantly get a unique shareable URL. Now you can:

  • Tap "Copy Link" to copy to clipboard
  • Tap "Share" to send via email, messaging, social media, etc.
  • Tap "QR Code" to generate a scannable QR code

Share the link however you want. Recipients don't need an account to download.

💡 Tip: After generating a link, it stays active according to your settings. You can view all your active shares in the "My Shares" tab.

Receiving & Downloading Files

If someone sent you an OmniDrop link, downloading is simple:

  1. Click or tap the link they sent you
  2. If password-protected, enter the password
  3. Tap "Download" or "Download All"
  4. Files download to your device's default download folder
  5. For mobile, files typically save to your Files app or camera roll

That's it! No account needed. You can download as many times as you want (until the limit is reached).

💡 Tip: If receiving many files, they may be automatically compressed (.zip). Uncompress them on your device after download.

Managing Your Shares

View Active Shares

Tap the "My Shares" tab to see all your active share links:

  • Share Link: The URL (tap to copy)
  • Expiration: When the link stops working
  • Downloads: How many times files have been downloaded (and remaining limit)
  • Status: Active, Expired, or Limit Reached

Monitor Download Activity

For each share, you can see:

  • Download timestamps (when someone accessed your files)
  • Number of downloads completed
  • Remaining downloads allowed

Delete or Expire a Share

To stop sharing files early:

  1. Find the share in "My Shares"
  2. Tap the three-dot menu (⋯)
  3. Select "Delete Link" or "Expire Now"
  4. Confirm—the link will no longer work
💡 Tip: Deleted links cannot be recovered. Recipients will see an error if they try to access them.

  • Password Protection: Optional passwords add an extra layer
  • Automatic Deletion: Files auto-delete after expiration
  • EU Storage: All files stored in Frankfurt for GDPR compliance

Privacy Practices

  • We don't track users or sell data

Troubleshooting

Upload is Slow

Upload speed depends on your internet connection and file size. To improve:

  • Check your internet speed (speedtest.net)
  • Try again at a different time
  • Compress large files before uploading (7-Zip, WinRAR, or built-in tools)
  • Use a wired connection for faster uploads (if possible)

Download Failed

If a recipient reports a download failed:

  • Check if the link is still active (hasn't expired)
  • Verify the download limit hasn't been reached
  • Ask them to try a different browser
  • Try re-generating the link and sharing again

Link Isn't Working

If a share link shows "Not Found" or "Expired":

  • Check the link expiration date (has it passed?)
  • Verify the download limit hasn't been reached
  • Ensure you copied the full link correctly
  • Try regenerating the link

Can't Log In

If you forgot your password:

  1. Tap "Forgot Password" on the login screen
  2. Enter your email address
  3. Check your email for a reset link
  4. Follow the link to create a new password

Best Practices

For Sensitive Files

  • Always use password protection
  • Set a short expiration (1-7 days)
  • Limit downloads to the number of recipients
  • Share the password separately (not in the link)

For Large Files

  • Compress files to reduce size (ZIP format)
  • Split very large uploads into parts
  • Test download speeds with a test file first
  • Consider email or cloud alternatives for 100GB+ files

For Business Use

  • Use a team account for shared responsibility
  • Set consistent expiration policies
  • Monitor download activity regularly
  • Use password protection for confidential documents
  • Keep receipts of shared information for compliance

Keyboard Shortcuts (Web & Desktop)

  • Ctrl+O (Cmd+O): Open file picker
  • Ctrl+C (Cmd+C): Copy share link
  • Ctrl+K (Cmd+K): Create new share
  • Escape: Close dialogs
  • Enter: Confirm actions
