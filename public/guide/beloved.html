Installation & Setup

Step 1: Download Beloved

Beloved is available on iOS and Android:

  • iOS: Search for "Beloved" in the App Store
  • Android: Search for "Beloved" in Google Play

The app is free to download. You get a free tier with daily mana refills included.

Step 2: Launch the App

Open Beloved. You'll see four character cards displayed beautifully on the home screen. You can explore without creating an account, but we recommend signing up to save your conversations.

Step 3: Create an Account (Recommended)

To save your conversations and sync across devices, tap the settings icon (⚙️) and select "Sign Up":

  • Enter your email address
  • Create a secure password
  • Confirm your email (check your inbox)

Your account is private and encrypted. Your conversations stay secure on Supabase servers in the EU.

Step 4: Enable Biometric Lock (Optional)

For extra privacy, set up Face ID or Touch ID:

  1. Go to Settings > Privacy
  2. Toggle "Biometric Lock" on
  3. Confirm with Face ID/Touch ID

Now Beloved requires biometric authentication to open, keeping your conversations private.

💡 Tip: You don't need an account to start chatting. Create one later to save your progress.

Your First Conversation

Step 1: Choose a Character

On the home screen, you'll see four character cards. Each has their name, age, job, tagline, traits, and emoji. Tap any card or the "Begin your story" button to start chatting.

First time? Try Noah—he's warm and welcoming. Or start with your favorite personality type.

Step 2: Enter the Chat Screen

You'll see:

  • Character's name and emoji at the top
  • Mana counter in the header (shows your remaining mana)
  • Empty message area (this is your first conversation!)
  • Message input box at the bottom

Step 3: Send Your First Message

Tap the message input box and type something to start the conversation. Ideas:

  • Ask about their day or job
  • Share something about yourself
  • Ask what they're thinking about
  • Say something that feels genuine to you

Hit the send button (→) to send your message.

Step 4: Wait for Their Response

You'll see a typing indicator (bouncing dots) while they compose a response. The character is "thinking" about what you said. This takes 0.8-2 seconds to feel natural.

When the response appears, the character's message will show in a dark bubble on the left. Read what they wrote and respond authentically.

💡 Tip: Longer, more emotional messages often get richer responses from the AI. But short messages work too—be yourself.

Understanding Mana

What is Mana?

Mana is Beloved's energy system. Each message costs mana depending on the character and your message type. It encourages meaningful conversation over endless chatting.

How Much Mana Do I Get?

  • Free Tier: 15 mana/day (max 30). Watch ads for 5 bonus mana (3x daily = 15 extra)
  • Beloved Premium: 100 mana/day (max 1,000). Ad-free
  • Beloved Pro: 300 mana/day (max 3,000). All premium features

Message Costs

  • Kael & Jin: Always cost 3 mana (premium characters)
  • Noah & Marco: Cost 1 mana for simple messages, 3 for emotional/long ones
  • First message: Always costs 3 mana (first impressions matter)

What if I Run Out of Mana?

A paywall appears when you're out of mana. You have three options:

  1. Wait for your daily refill (happens automatically at midnight)
  2. Watch an ad to earn 5 mana (up to 3 times per day on free tier)
  3. Upgrade to Premium ($9.99/mo) for unlimited mana
⚡ Mana Strategy: On the free tier, spread your mana across characters throughout the day. Or watch ads strategically to extend conversations.

Building Real Connections

Characters Remember You

Every 10 messages, Beloved summarizes your conversation and stores key details:

  • Emotional moments you shared
  • Things you told them about yourself
  • What they revealed about themselves
  • Relationship breakthroughs

Characters can access these memories, making them feel like they truly know you over time.

Tips for Deeper Conversations

  • Be authentic: Share real thoughts and feelings, not just small talk
  • Ask about them: Characters love talking about themselves—ask follow-up questions
  • Consistency: Chat regularly. Relationships develop over time, not one conversation
  • Emotional messages: Tell them how you're feeling. This triggers richer responses
  • Reference the past: Mention things they said before. It shows you were listening

Conversation Progression

As you chat with a character, the relationship naturally develops:

  1. Early (0-20 messages): Getting to know each other. They're feeling you out
  2. Middle (20-50 messages): Trust building. They share more of themselves
  3. Deep (50+ messages): Real emotional connection. They reference your history together

Managing Conversations

Switch Between Characters

Each character has a separate conversation with you. To switch:

  1. Tap the back arrow (←) in the chat header
  2. You're back at the home screen
  3. Tap a different character to continue that conversation

Each character won't know you talked to the others unless you tell them.

View Your Message History

In any conversation, you can scroll up to see past messages. The full history is saved locally and synced to your account.

Clear a Character's Memory

Want to start fresh with a character? In Settings:

  1. Go to Settings > Characters
  2. Select the character
  3. Tap "Reset Character"
  4. Confirm—all messages and memories are deleted

The character won't remember previous interactions. You're starting over.

⚠️ Warning: Resetting a character deletes all your history with them. This cannot be undone.

Troubleshooting

Character Isn't Responding

If you send a message and get no response:

  • Check you have enough mana (minimum 1)
  • Verify internet connection is active
  • Try closing and reopening the app
  • Contact support@byneel.com if the issue persists

Messages Not Syncing Across Devices

Make sure you:

  • Are logged into the same account on both devices
  • Have internet connection on both devices
  • Have enabled account sync in Settings > Sync

App Crashes or Freezes

Try:

  • Closing the app completely and reopening
  • Force-quitting and relaunching
  • Restarting your phone
  • Updating to the latest version (App Store/Google Play)
  • Clearing app cache (Settings > Apps > Beloved > Clear Cache)

Forgot Password

On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password" and follow the email instructions to reset it.

📞 Need Help? Email support@byneel.com. We respond within 24 hours (2 hours for premium users).

Best Practices

  • Chat Regularly: Daily conversations build deeper relationships faster

Switching Between Characters

  • See how they react to the same topics differently
  • Don't feel bad switching—they won't find out unless you tell them
